Reaper Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 9, 2026 · Last updated: July 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Reaper ("Reaper," "we," "our," or "us") handles information when a user connects a TikTok account to Reaper. Reaper is designed to use TikTok's official APIs only after the user completes TikTok's authorization process.

1. Information we process

Depending on the permissions a user chooses to authorize, Reaper may process:

  • Basic TikTok profile information, including open ID, display name, and avatar.
  • Profile details authorized through TikTok, currently limited to the account biography.
  • Account statistics, including follower, following, likes, and video counts.
  • Public video metadata and performance information available through the authorized video.list scope.
  • OAuth authorization information, including access and refresh tokens needed to maintain the connection.
  • Content-posting data supplied by the user, including local video bytes streamed to TikTok without permanent Reaper object storage; verified video or photo source URLs; media size and photo count; optional titles and descriptions; TikTok server-side publish identifiers; processing status; and bounded failure information.
  • Technical and security records needed to operate, troubleshoot, and protect the service, including Reaper user and organization identifiers, event type, provider operation identifiers, counts, outcomes, and timestamps. Tokens, one-time upload URLs, and local filenames are not written to audit metadata.

Reaper does not receive or store a user's TikTok password.

2. How we use information

We use authorized information to connect the user's TikTok account, display profile and performance analytics, provide requested content workflows, maintain service security, diagnose failures, and comply with legal obligations. We do not use TikTok data for unrelated advertising or sell TikTok user data.

3. Authorization and user choice

Access is granted through TikTok Login Kit. TikTok displays the requested permissions before authorization. Users may decline authorization or revoke Reaper's access through TikTok account settings. Features that depend on a revoked permission will stop working.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only with TikTok as necessary to use TikTok APIs, infrastructure providers acting on our behalf under appropriate safeguards, when directed by the user, or when required by law or needed to protect users and the service.

5. Data retention and deletion

Raw OAuth state expires within 10 minutes and is stored only as a hash. One-time TikTok upload capabilities are encrypted and cleared within one hour. Disconnecting TikTok deletes the local connection, encrypted provider tokens, and associated upload records. Security audit records—including event type, Reaper user and organization identifiers, provider publish identifier when applicable, counts, outcomes, and timestamps—may remain for up to 90 days after the event for abuse prevention, incident response, and compliance, then are deleted by the scheduled retention job unless a longer period is legally required. Users can request access or deletion through the contact method below; verified requests are honored subject to necessary legal retention.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect OAuth tokens and account information, including restricted access, encrypted transport, tenant-scoped access controls, and credential isolation. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. International processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than the user's own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

8. Children

Reaper is not directed to children under 13, or a higher minimum age where required by local law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

9. Third-party services

TikTok's handling of information is governed by TikTok's own terms and privacy policies. Reaper is not responsible for third-party services outside our control.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the service or legal requirements change. The updated date above will identify the current version. Material changes will be communicated when required.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, access requests, or deletion requests, email Reaper Privacy Support. Do not include passwords or access tokens.
