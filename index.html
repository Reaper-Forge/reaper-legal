Reaper

Reaper helps authorized TikTok users understand their public profile, audience statistics, and published video performance through TikTok's official APIs. Users connect their own account through TikTok Login Kit and control the permissions they grant.

User control: Reaper never asks for a TikTok password. Access is granted through TikTok's OAuth authorization screen and can be revoked by the user.
