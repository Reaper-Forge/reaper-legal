User Data Deletion

Effective date: July 12, 2026 · Last updated: July 12, 2026

You may request deletion of data associated with your use of Reaper Legion at any time.

How to disconnect access

  1. Remove Reaper Legion from the connected-app, Business Integration, or Apps and Websites settings of the applicable Meta, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, or other platform account.
  2. Revoking access prevents new API collection but does not by itself guarantee deletion of information already stored by the application.

How to request deletion

  1. Email sargesgaming@gmail.com with the subject Reaper Legion Data Deletion Request.
  2. Identify the platform connection you want removed and provide the minimum account identifier needed to locate the record, such as your platform username or app-scoped user ID. Do not send passwords, access tokens, backup codes, or government identification.
  3. We will acknowledge the request and may ask for a safe ownership confirmation if needed to prevent unauthorized deletion.

Deletion timeline

Verified requests are targeted for completion within 30 days. We delete stored OAuth tokens, connected-account identifiers, imported profile/content metadata, cached insights, and other application data associated with the connection, except information that must be retained temporarily for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or documented backup rotation.

Confirmation

When deletion is complete, we will send confirmation to the requesting email address. Questions may be sent to sargesgaming@gmail.com.
